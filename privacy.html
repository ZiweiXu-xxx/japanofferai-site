Privacy Policy

How JapanOffer AI collects, uses and protects your personal data, and the rights you have under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). The English version of this page is the governing version.

Last updated: 28 November 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

JapanOffer AI is the data controller for the personal data collected through japanofferai.com. Contact us about anything in this policy at hello@japanofferai.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and login credentials when you sign up or sign in.
  • Profile and job-search information you provide: your background, skills, target roles and markets, CV or profile text, saved jobs, application notes and deadlines.
  • Reports: the match reports generated for you and your remaining credit balance.
  • Usage data: pages you visit and actions you take on the site, collected for analytics and to improve the service.
  • Payment records: what you bought, when, and how much — processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • Messages you send us: emails and feedback you send to us.

We do not ask for sensitive personal data (such as health information, religion or political views). Please do not include it in your profile or CV text.

3. Why we use your data

  • To run the service and your account, including generating match reports — this is necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To process payments and manage credits and subscriptions — contract.
  • To improve the service and fix problems, using usage data — our legitimate interest in running a working product.
  • To reply to your messages and send service notices about your account — contract and legitimate interest.
  • To meet legal duties, for example keeping payment records — legal obligation.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for advertising by third parties.

4. Who we share it with

We share data only with the services needed to run the site:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and authentication, and stores your account, profile and report data.
  • Stripe — processes your payments. Stripe receives your payment details directly; we receive only the payment result and records.
  • OpenAI — the profile and job information needed to generate your match report is sent to OpenAI’s API when you request a report. OpenAI does not use API data to train its models.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK and the European Economic Area. Where that happens, transfers rely on the safeguards the law requires, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses. We will also share data if the law requires it, or to protect the service from abuse.

5. Member network and employers

If you choose to make your profile visible in the member network or to employers, other members or employers can see the profile information you chose to share. Anything you put in a public or shared part of the site may be seen by others; keep private details out of it.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and profile data while your account is active. If you close your account, we delete or anonymise your profile and report data within a reasonable time, except for records we must keep by law (for example payment records) and for as long as needed to resolve a dispute. Usage and analytics data is kept in aggregated form.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have your data deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. Email hello@japanofferai.com to exercise any of these rights; we will respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) if you think we have handled your data unfairly.

8. Cookies

We use cookies and local storage for things the site needs to work: keeping you signed in, remembering your language choice, and basic usage analytics. There are no third-party advertising cookies. You can clear cookies in your browser settings; signing in will not work without the essential ones.

9. Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections, access controls on our database, and by never storing card details. No system is completely secure; if you believe your account has been compromised, tell us at hello@japanofferai.com and change your password.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or the law changes. If a change is material, we will tell you through the service or by email before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

11. Contact

Questions about your data or this policy: hello@japanofferai.com. See also our Terms of Service and Refund Policy.
