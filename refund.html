Refund Policy

When you can get a refund for match report credits or Premium, and how to ask for one. The English version of this page is the governing version.

Last updated: 28 November 2026

1. What you are buying

JapanOffer AI sells digital content and services: single match reports for £4.99 (1 credit), packs of 5 reports for £14.99 (5 credits), and Premium at £19.99 per month (20 reports per billing month). Your first match report is free. Job search itself is free. Payments are processed by Stripe, and JapanOffer AI never sees or stores your card details.

2. Your 14-day cooling-off right

If you buy online as a consumer, you normally have 14 days from the purchase to change your mind and cancel for a full refund. There is one important exception for digital content: once you have expressly agreed that we can supply the digital content immediately, and you have acknowledged that you will lose the right to cancel once supply begins, that right ends when the content is supplied. Generating a match report, or using a credit, is the point at which supply begins.

In practice this means:

  • Credits you have not used: if you bought a single report or a pack and have not generated a report with those credits, you can cancel within 14 days of purchase for a full refund of the unused credits.
  • Reports already generated: once a report has been generated for you, that credit has been supplied and is not refundable under the cooling-off right.
  • Premium: you can cancel within 14 days of starting Premium for a refund, but only for the part of the month you have not used. If you cancel after the 14 days, Premium stays active until the end of the billing month you have already paid for, and that month is not refunded.

3. Faults and billing mistakes

Separate from the cooling-off right, tell us if something went wrong and we will put it right:

  • a report failed to generate but a credit was taken;
  • you were charged twice, or charged the wrong amount;
  • Premium did not activate after a successful payment.

In these cases we will reissue the credit or report where possible, and refund the amount affected where a reissue is not possible. Contact us within 14 days of the problem so we can check the payment and report records while they are fresh.

4. What is not refundable

  • Credits already used to generate a report, unless the report failed to generate (see section 3).
  • A Premium month that has already started, except for the unused part within the first 14 days (see section 2).
  • A report you disagree with: match reports are AI-generated guidance based on the information you provided, not a promise of a job or interview, so a low score or a suggestion you do not like is not a fault.

5. How to ask for a refund

Email hello@japanofferai.com with the subject “Refund request” and include your account email, the date and amount of the purchase, and what happened. We will reply within 3 working days. If we agree a refund, it goes back to the original payment method through Stripe, normally within 14 days of us agreeing it, and usually much sooner.

6. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights as a consumer under UK law, including the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. If any part of this policy conflicts with those rights, your statutory rights apply.

7. Contact

Refund questions: hello@japanofferai.com. See also our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
