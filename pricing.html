How billing works

Your reports, your balance.

Your first match report is free. After that, each match report uses one credit: single reports and packs add credits to your balance, and Premium gives you 20 reports every billing month. Your remaining balance is shown on the report pages and on your account page. Payments are processed by Stripe; JapanOffer AI never sees or stores your card details.
