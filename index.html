Questions

Before you start.

Is the first match report really free?

Yes. Every new account gets one free match report. After that, each report uses one credit — buy a single report, a 5-report pack, or go Premium for 20 reports a month. Prices are listed in the pricing section above.

What does a match report cover?

A structured report on your target market, role direction, match score, visa and language risk, application priority and a cover-letter opening. It is a rule-based assessment of the information you provide — not a human review, and not a guarantee of an interview or an offer.

Which markets and roles does it cover?

Japan, the United Kingdom, Hong Kong, Singapore, Canada, Australia and remote roles, with a focus on legal, compliance, finance, AML, KYC, Web3 and international business.

Do I need a card for the free report?

No. Creating an account and generating your first match report needs no payment method. You only pay when you choose to buy more reports or Premium.

How does Premium work?

Premium gives you 20 match reports every billing month plus premium job-search features. Cancel any time from your account page — Premium stays active until the end of the current billing period. The monthly price is shown in the pricing section above.
